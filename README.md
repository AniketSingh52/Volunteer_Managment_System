# 🌍 Volunteer Management System

A web-based platform designed to simplify and digitize the process of connecting **organizations** and **volunteers** for various **social welfare programs** such as cleanliness drives, animal rescue, awareness campaigns, and more. The system provides a seamless interface for event creation, volunteer registration, and real-time interaction.

---

## 📌 Project Objective

The objective of this system is to:
- Provide a centralized platform for organizations to post volunteer events.
- Allow volunteers to easily browse, search, and apply for events.
- Enhance engagement through social features like **chat**, **post sharing**, and **commenting**.
- Reduce manual effort and time spent in volunteer coordination.
- Digitize volunteer management for NGOs, educational, and commercial institutions (initially limited to Mumbai).

---

## ✨ Features

- 👥 **User Registration & Login**  
  Secure registration for organizations and volunteers with profile management.

- 🗓️ **Event Creation & Management**  
  Organizations can create, update, and manage volunteer events.

- 🔍 **Event Search & Matching**  
  Volunteers can browse and apply to events based on interests, location, and skills.

- 💬 **Chat System**  
  Real-time **personal and group messaging** for coordination and interaction.

- 📸 **Post Pictures**  
  Users can post images of events, activities, or campaigns they participated in.

- 💬 **Commenting on Posts**  
  Volunteers and organizations can engage by commenting on shared images.

- ⭐ **Event Review and Rating**  
  Volunteers can leave feedback and rate events they've attended.

- 📁 **Profile Management**  
  Users can update their profile, reset password, and manage preferences.

- 🛡️ **Admin Panel**  
  Admin can monitor users, events, posts, and has the ability to suspend/unsuspend content or users.
